# Chapter 6

The kiss on the lips came at the end of the night, quick and closed-mouthed, and she felt it more than she felt the warmth of it. It wasn’t meant for her, she knew that. Karen’s name hung in the air afterward, and when Barry remarked that her perfume smelt nicer, she didn’t protest. She didn’t know how to protest something that had never belonged to her in the first place.

Jealousy settled in her chest like damp — heavy, breathless, impossible to air out. She said nothing. She listened instead, and later, after she’d gone home, he told her that he’d enjoyed pleasuring himself before sleep. The admission was casual, almost tender in its honesty, and it gave her enough courage to ask the question she’d been turning over in her mind.

“Do you ever think of me when you do that?”

He didn’t hesitate. “No.”

The answer didn’t wound her as much as it should have. It was clean, at least. No false comfort. No softening of edges just to spare her feelings. She respected that, in its own strange way.

She began to talk about moving a few things over — her bits, her small things — into his flat. She was specific about the underwear drawer. She wanted to share it. She didn’t want to wear her own.

He liked the idea. He liked her company, he said. But she could hear it in his voice, the undertone of distraction: he was still happier thinking about his date. The knowledge didn’t break her, but it sat there, a quiet thing she carried without complaint.

Then she asked him about fantasies.

His answer came slowly, as if he were reaching for something simple and safe. He sounded very vanilla — just to make love with a woman. Nothing complicated, nothing theatrical. Just that.

She didn’t fancy him, not really. There were things that got in the way — his eyes, his beard, the fact of his Willy, or lack of it — things she couldn’t quite look past. But fancying him wasn’t the same as wanting him, and wanting him wasn’t the same as the pull she felt toward the idea of him.

Later, in his bath, the water warm around her, she let her mind drift. She thought about him. She touched herself slowly, quietly, while he was in another room, while the house was quiet and he wasn’t watching. It was enough, just for that moment, to have the thought of him there and to let it carry her somewhere soft and private.
